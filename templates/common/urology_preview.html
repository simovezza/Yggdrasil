{% icon 'microscope' %} Urology Clinical Workspace

Dedicated research workspace for urological oncology and surgical planning. Built around two core imaging modalities: Whole Slide Imaging (WSI) for digital histopathology and Multiparametric Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) for volumetric tissue analysis.

{% icon 'layer-group' %} Core Clinical Modalities

{% icon 'microscope' %}

WSI

Whole Slide Imaging
Digital Pathology

Gigapixel digital pathology for prostate, kidney, and bladder histopathology. Deep-zoom tissue exploration with multi-level slide pyramids, AI boundary segmentation, and durable Gleason grading.

  • Multi-resolution pyramid viewer for ultra-high-resolution biopsy and resection slides
  • Automated Gleason pattern classification and glandular architecture detection
  • Durable multi-reader annotations for tumor margins, stroma, and lymphovascular invasion
Formats: .svs, .ndpi, .mrxs, .tiff, DeepZoom
Gigapixel Pyramid Gleason Grading Tissue Margins Biopsy Cores
{% icon 'file-waveform' %}

MRI

Magnetic Resonance Imaging
Volumetric Radiology

Multiparametric MRI (mpMRI) review for prostate lesion detection and renal tumor volumetry. Spatial co-registration across anatomical and functional sequences with Cornerstone3D orthogonal MPR.

  • Synchronized viewports across T2W, Diffusion-Weighted Imaging (DWI), and ADC maps
  • PI-RADS v2.1 structured reporting with automated peripheral and transition zone segmentation
  • Volumetric renal tumor delineation and 3D surface mesh export for nephron-sparing surgery
Formats: .nii.gz (NIfTI), multi-sequence volumes
T2W / DWI / ADC PI-RADS v2.1 Cornerstone3D MPR 3D Volumetry
